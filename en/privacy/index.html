Privacy

Spinsel · iPad · November 6, 2026

No ads. No third-party analytics. No child account. Drawings stay on your iPad. Purchases go through Apple.

What stays on the iPad

Profiles, drawings, album, progress, and owned packs — so the app works offline. None of that is sent to our servers.

Purchases

Apple handles payment. We never see your card or Apple ID password — only that a pack was bought, so we can unlock it.

What we don’t do

Third-party ads, cross-app tracking, selling data, or “sign up for stats.”

For parents

Prices appear only after a parental gate. A child’s drawings aren’t a product: finished work stays viewable after a refund. New paid packs stay locked without entitlement.

Deleting data

Delete the app and local data usually goes with it. A new iPad won’t carry the album over — export first if it matters. Restore Purchases brings packs back, not drawings.

This site

Privacy and support only. No ad cookies, no login. If you email us, we see your address and message so we can reply.

Changes

If something changes, we’ll update the date here and the App Store privacy answers.
