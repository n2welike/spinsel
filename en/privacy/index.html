Privacy

Spinsel · iPad · November 9, 2026

We collect no data. No ads. No third-party analytics. No child account. Drawings stay on your iPad. Purchases go through Apple.

What stays on the iPad

Profiles (a name or picture you choose), drawings, album, progress, settings, and which packs you own — so the app works offline. None of that is sent to us or anyone else.

Downloading new drawings

The app downloads its story list and drawing packs from our own file storage on Google Cloud. It’s a plain file download: no account, no identifiers, nothing about your child. Like any web server, the storage sees the iPad’s IP address to deliver the file; we don’t log or keep it.

Children’s privacy

Spinsel is made for children aged 6–8. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else, and we don’t ask for it. Links outside the app, sharing, saving to Photos, rating the app, and any purchases sit behind a parental gate — a math question for grown-ups.

Purchases

Spinsel includes a free sample. More can be unlocked with one-time purchases: the Base Workshop and separate story packs. No subscription, no in-game currency, no random rewards. Apple handles payment; the app only checks Apple’s confirmation on the iPad to unlock a pack. We never see your card, Apple ID, or any personal details. Prices and Buy buttons appear only after the parental gate.

What we don’t do

Third-party ads, cross-app tracking, selling data, or “sign up for stats.”

For parents

A child’s drawings aren’t a product: finished work stays viewable no matter what. Saving to Photos happens only when a grown-up asks for it.

Deleting data

Delete the app and local data usually goes with it. A new iPad won’t carry the album over — export first if it matters. Restore Purchases brings packs back, not drawings.

This site

Privacy and support only. No ad cookies, no login. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP when you open this site. If you email us, we see your address and message so we can reply.

Who we are

Spinsel is made by Anatolii Solovev, an independent developer. Questions about privacy: n2we.nlike@gmail.com.

Changes

If something changes, we’ll update the date here and the App Store privacy answers.
