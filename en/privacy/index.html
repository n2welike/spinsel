Privacy Policy

Spinsel for iPad · Date: November 6, 2026

In short. Spinsel is a kids’ app. We do not show ads, do not use third-party analytics, and do not create child accounts. Drawings and profiles stay on your iPad. Purchases are handled by Apple.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Spinsel app on the App Store. For questions, use the support page.

2. Who the app is for

Spinsel is designed for children about ages 5–8 and follows App Store Kids Category rules. Purchase decisions belong to an adult.

3. What data exists in the app

On the device

  • child profiles (name, age path, and settings you choose);
  • drawings, album, stickers, and progress;
  • confirmed purchase entitlements so content works offline and after relaunch;
  • a local event diary (on the iPad only, for path understanding and debugging).

This data is not sent to Spinsel servers and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive card numbers, Apple ID passwords, or other payment details. Apple may show us that a product was purchased so we can unlock content.

Network

The app may contact Apple services for purchases, restore, and the system review prompt. If signed content packs are later downloaded, that transfer will not include children’s names, drawings, or contact details. There is no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics.

4. What we do not do

  • require a child account;
  • show third-party ads;
  • use third-party analytics SDKs;
  • sell data;
  • track users across apps and websites.

5. Parents and purchases

Prices and payment appear only after a parental gate. Restore Purchases lives in the grown-ups area. A child’s artwork is not a product: after a confirmed Apple refund, earlier drawings can still be viewed and exported; new paid content stays locked without entitlement.

6. Storage and deletion

Data lives in the app container on the iPad. Deleting the app usually deletes local data. Export important drawings before changing devices. Restoring purchases does not restore the drawing album.

7. Children and the law

We design the app so it does not collect children’s personal data beyond what is needed to work on the device. Parents can write to us with questions. Family Sharing and Ask to Buy follow Apple Account settings.

8. This website

This site hosts the privacy policy and support information. It does not set advertising cookies and does not require sign-in. If you email support, we see your address and message so we can reply.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page. Material changes about purchases or data will also be reflected in App Store Connect (App Privacy).
