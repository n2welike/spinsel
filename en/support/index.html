Common questions

Purchase did not unlock

Open the grown-ups area → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that paid. Internet is required only for buying and restoring; owned content works offline.

Access missing on a new iPad

Restore purchases in the app. Drawings are stored locally and do not move automatically — export them first if they matter.

Can a child see the price?

No. Price and payment appear only after a parental gate. The child path has no buy button.

Is there a subscription?

No. Only one-time purchases with no auto-renewal.
